# Evidence: swimming, unique fish, plot taps, tools, house, corral, free placement (2026-11-22)

1. **Swimming**: walking into the lake or river now swims. The body sits low in the water, with breaststroke arms and kicking legs, and the farmer is slower. Swimming spends a little energy and nobody can fish while swimming. The rod hides in the water.
2. **Fish book**: 36 sprites were cut from the second sheet. With the 12 from the first, there are 48 in `assets/fish/`, and every one of the 35 species has its own picture with no tints. The rarer species get the fancier ones.
3. **Field**: tap a plot to plant it (pick the crop) or to harvest it when ripe. A growing plot shows its time left. The field screen keeps the crop list and buying plots.
4. **Tools**: cutting a tree needs the axe and breaking a rock needs the pickaxe. Both are bought once in Loja › Fazenda.
5. **House**: "Entrar em casa" at the door leads to a warm interior.
   - The room has a kitchen, table and chairs, a rug, a sofa, a floor lamp with an orange light, a window, a cupboard and the bed.
   - The bed sleeps until the next morning with full energy, and the sofa lets the farmer rest (the old Descansar button is gone).
   - The light inside is warm and dim on a dark background.
6. **Corral** (after the picture): packed-dirt floor with speckles and stones, a reddish fence, a grey-roofed barn with an X door and a water trough. The cows are blocky Holsteins with black patches, a pink snout, horns and black hooves.
7. **Free placement**: the coop, corral, factories, pig pen and sheep pen are built where the player chooses.
   - From Loja › Obras, a green or red footprint follows the farmer, and "Construir aqui" builds there.
   - It needs grass, flowers or sand; loose trees and rocks under the footprint are cleared.
   - The truck finds the closest road. Older saves keep their buildings at the old spots.
